01 / The reel

45 seconds. Zero wasted frames.

Built the way I build your ads: hook first, proof in the middle, a clear ask at the end.

  1. 0–3s

    Cold-open thumb-stop

    Your single most arresting frame. No logo, no intro. Motion in frame one.

  2. 3–12s

    Hook machine-gun

    6–8 of the best opening seconds back-to-back, cut on the beat. Proves you can hook any category.

  3. 12–25s

    Range: UGC → polished

    Talking-head UGC, product demo, unboxing, before/after, founder story. Split-screen 9:16s.

  4. 25–37s

    Proof overlays

    Hook rate, CTR and ROAS burned on screen over the ads that earned them.

  5. 37–45s

    The ask

    Name, one-line promise, "Book a strategy call" end card. Hard cut to black.

03 / The Lab Method

I run your creative like a science experiment.

Because that's where I learned to work. Hypothesis, controlled variables, measured results, repeat.

  1. H

    Hypothesis

    We pick one angle and one audience pain, and write down what we expect to win and why.

  2. V

    Variants

    3 hooks × 2 lengths per concept, one variable changed at a time, so we know what worked.

  3. T

    Test

    Files named for your Ads Manager (C04_H2_22s_UGC). Your buyer launches in minutes.

  4. I

    Iterate

    You send me the numbers. I cut the next round around the winning hook. Compounding wins.

06 / Objections, handled

Questions buyers ask before they book.

How fast do I get my first cuts?

48–72 hours from receiving raw footage and the brief. Need it for a launch tomorrow? Rush 24h delivery is available. Ask on the call.

What if I don't like the edit?

Every deliverable includes 2 revision rounds. In practice most edits land in one, because we agree on the hypothesis and hook before I touch the timeline.

Can you handle real testing volume?

Yes. Retainers start at 20+ variants a month and scale with your spend. Files come named and organized for Ads Manager so your buyer can launch immediately.

Do you only edit, or help with strategy too?

Both. I'll suggest angles, hooks and scripts from your reviews and comments, and brief your creators on the shots I need to make the edit work.

What do you need from me?

Raw footage (or creator access), your brand guidelines, current best-performing ad, and the metric you care about most. That's it.

Can you make French or Mandarin versions?

Yes. I'm fluent in English and French and speak Mandarin, so I can cut native-feeling variants for Québec, France and Chinese-speaking audiences, not just translated captions.

Do you work with agencies / white-label?

Yes. I plug into your workflow, naming conventions and project tools, and never contact your clients directly.
